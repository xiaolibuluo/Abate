# Contributor License Agreement (CLA · Draft)

> **Draft**: this document is a draft pending review; **whether it is required is subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**.
> **This document does not constitute legal advice / 本文件不构成法律意见; subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel.**
> This document **does not change the CLA from optional to mandatory**; current governance remains "CLA optional, DCO mandatory".

## 1. Status and Positioning

- Existing `governance/DUAL-LICENSE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` record "**CLA optional, DCO mandatory**"; this draft **does not change that status**.
- This draft is only a textual skeleton prepared for the case in which the rights holder elects to adopt a CLA. It is **not effective, not signed, and grants no rights**; it contains no already-signed / paid / certified / endorsed claims.

## 2. Why It Is Discussed in a Dual-Licensing Context (subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel)

- **The DCO does not grant the project a relicensing right**; under dual licensing (AGPLv3 + commercial), having the project exercise relicensing uniformly may require a CLA or an explicit relicensing clause.
- That gap is **subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**; this draft **does not decide the matter and does not make the CLA mandatory**.

## 3. Draft Clause Skeleton (each item subject to confirmation by the rights holder)

1. **Rights statement**: the contributor certifies legitimate rights over submitted material and the right to submit it under the project license.
2. **Copyright license grant (wording TBD)**: an intended grant to the project to use, copy, modify, and distribute the contribution; **the exact scope and whether relicensing is included are subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**.
3. **Patent license (inclusion TBD)**: whether a patent grant is included, its scope and exceptions, **subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**.
4. **Relationship with the DCO**: DCO 1.1 remains the mandatory sign-off (see `DCO.md`); if a CLA is enabled, its **optional / mandatory** status is **subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel** and is not preset by this draft.
5. **Dual-licensing application**: how contributions are used across the AGPLv3 and commercial paths is **subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**; this draft fabricates nothing.
6. **Effectiveness and withdrawal**: effectiveness conditions, signing method, and withdrawal mechanism are **undetermined** and **subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**.
