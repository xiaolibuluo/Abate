# Community Code of Conduct (CODE_OF_CONDUCT)

> This document states only the basic expectations for community collaboration. It **sets no SLA, fabricates no response / handling timeframes or escalation workflow**, and invents no governing body or membership list.
> **This document does not constitute legal advice / 本文件不构成法律意见; subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel.**

## 1. Scope

- Applies to all participants in the project's issues, contributions, discussions, and reviews.
- Whether a separate enforcement body, appeal process, and concrete handling rules exist is **subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**; this document **fabricates none**.

## 2. Expected Behavior

- Respect and inclusion: discuss on the basis of facts and technology; avoid personal attacks, discrimination, and harassment.
- Honest wording: describe capabilities / versions / competitors / licenses on the basis of verifiable commands, real files, and measured data; `competitorAbsolute = NOT_MEASURED` and `NOT_MEASURED` are retained verbatim; no absolute competitor claims.
- Compliant contributions: follow the DCO 1.1 sign-off and dependency allowlist in `CONTRIBUTING.md` / `DCO.md`.
- Respect for licensing: follow the dual-licensing arrangement in `LICENSING.md`; legal determinations are **subject to confirmation by the rights holder or legal counsel**.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks; spreading unverified capability / performance / competitor / licensing claims.
- Introducing GPL / LGPL / AGPL variant dependencies, forging sign-offs, bypassing gates, tampering with frozen files.
- Any fabricated certification / endorsement / pricing / signed-contract / SLA claims.
